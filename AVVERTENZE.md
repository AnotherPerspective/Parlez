## In full

**What it is.** Parlez! connects After Effects to an artificial intelligence (AI) assistant of your choice, for example Claude. When it is on, the assistant can read the open project and change it: create and edit layers, compositions, keyframes and effects, save files. To understand the project it sends the assistant names, text, file paths and frames.

**Free, experimental, open source.** Parlez! is free and still in development: it may contain bugs, change, or stop working after an update of After Effects or of the AI service. It is open source, under the MIT License: the text is in the [LICENSE](LICENSE) file. These notices take nothing away from the MIT License: they add to it.

**As is.** You get it as is, without any warranty: no promise that it works, that it suits your work or that it is free of errors.

**At your own risk.** Using it is your choice, and the consequences are yours: changed, damaged or lost projects, working hours, late deliveries, data sent to the AI service.

**Liability.** Another Perspective Pictures SRL and the authors of Parlez!, including those of third-party components, are not liable for damages caused by using Parlez! or by it not working, as far as the law allows.

**Your data.** Parlez! on its own does not connect to the internet. But what it reads from the project reaches the AI assistant you use, and from there the service that runs it (for Claude, Anthropic), under the rules of your plan. For work under NDA or otherwise confidential, check those rules first. Parlez!'s confidential mode hides names and paths, but it does not replace your own judgment.

**For professionals.** Parlez! is meant for graphics and video professionals who are used to working on copies and keeping backups.

**With care.** Work on a copy of the project, never on a client's only original. Make a restore point before risky work, save often, and keep the panel OFF when you do not need it.

**Future versions.** These notices apply to this version and to the next ones, including when Parlez! is no longer "beta", until we publish new ones.

**Acceptance.** By installing or using Parlez! you accept these terms. `parlez setup` asks you once, before installing.
